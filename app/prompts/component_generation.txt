The preview and final render use a 1920x1080 frame. Design for that actual canvas and keep the complete composition inside it.

- Use AutoFitText for variable-length display copy. Give text containers a bounded width, set minWidth: 0 on grid/flex children, and allow natural wrapping. Do not rely on guessed text widths, nowrap, or fixed-height text containers.

- Keep the focal visual and its essential labels visible together through the main reading window.
